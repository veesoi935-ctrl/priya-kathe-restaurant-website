Our Living History

Nine Decades of Slow Cooking & Soulful Food

When our founders migrated to Delhi, they brought along only two treasures: ancestral brass utensils and closely guarded spice formulations. That was the genesis of Kake Da Hotel.

Today, our Malviya Nagar kitchen remains faithful to those foundational rituals. We never rush our curries; the meat is hand-tenderized and slow-cooked in curd for hours without artificial thickeners.

Wood & Real Coal Tandoors
We refuse gas-powered simulated ovens. Every piece of chicken, mutton seekh, and bread is baked against red-hot clay walls heated strictly with babool coal wood.
32-Spice Ancestral Potli Formula
Whole Kashmiri chili, green cardamom, mace, nutmeg, and black cloves are stone-pounded fresh every morning to preserve natural essential oils.
Pure Desi Ghee Simmering
Our gravies are prepared strictly in farm-churned clarified butter (Desi Ghee). No palm oils or commercial gravy pastes are ever allowed into our brass deghs.
Atithi Devo Bhava Hospitality
Every guest is family. Generous Punjabi portions, sizzling hot service, and customized spice tolerances ensure unmatched culinary satisfaction.
